# Martinez logo

`martinez-logo.svg` is the unmodified logo served by the City of Martinez's official website, including its 2026 sesquicentennial seal.

- Official website: https://www.cityofmartinez.org/
- Original asset: https://www.cityofmartinez.org/Project/Contents/Main/_gfx/cmn/logo-new.svg
- Retrieved: September 9, 2026

The logo identifies the agency in this demonstration and remains the city's branding.
